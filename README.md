# WesSciJo: Website Concepts

Five custom WordPress themes built for [The Wesleyan Science Journal](https://wesleyan.edu), a new hybrid peer-reviewed journal / science magazine at Wesleyan University. Built as Lead Web Designer to pitch structurally distinct directions for the journal's launch site, including two hybrids that recombine elements from the first three.

Each theme is a from-scratch WordPress build: custom `header.php`, `index.php`, `single.php`, `footer.php`, `functions.php`, and `style.css`, no page builder, no purchased theme. The brand palette (`#D72121` red, black, `#CB9A3B` gold) was measured directly from wesleyan.edu's live stylesheet instead of guessed. The university's actual display and serif faces, Replica Pro and Copernicus, are commercially licensed and unavailable here, so Libre Franklin stands in. It's also part of Wesleyan's own font stack and free on Google Fonts.

## The five concepts

| | Theme | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `wessci-dark` | **Dark Laboratory**, or Swiss Style: near-black ground, red as a surgical accent, hover nav panels, monospaced metadata |
| 2 | `wessci-brutal` | **Editorial Brutalist**: red slabs, condensed display caps, a permanent index strip that exposes every section without hovering |
| 3 | `wessci-broadsheet` | **Archival Broadsheet**, or Editorial: warm off-white, classical serif, centred masthead, a persistent left index rail with live article counts |
| 4 | `wessci-hybrid-a` | **Hybrid A**: black-to-white hero at Brutalist scale in Swiss's typeface, Brutalist-formatted index on white, Swiss-layout featured article, toned-down Brutalist body |
| 5 | `wessci-hybrid-b` | **Hybrid B**: same hero, index, and featured article as Hybrid A, but with an even 3-column card grid instead of the asymmetric one, dropping the wide featured card for a calmer, uniform rhythm |

All five share the same structural requirements from the brief: article cards showing type + title + cover art, a prominent Current Issue, accessible Archives, and navigation organized around the journal's two divisions (Research & Reviews / News, Features & Perspectives) with article types nested beneath each.

## Status

This is concept work, not the final site. The articles and cover art in the screenshots are placeholder content, generated for structural review rather than real journal content.
