The Wesleyan Science Journal will serve as a hybrid model between a traditional peer-reviewed scientific journal and a science magazine. As such, we aim to publish a variety of content, including journal articles, review articles, interviews, and personal essays. Our content is organized into two overarching themes: Research & Reviews and News, Features, & Perspectives.

Article Content Guidelines

Comprehensive instructions on article structure, formatting, discipline-specific citation styles, and editorial policies for all submission categories (Journal Articles, News and Views, Literature Reviews, News, Features, and Op/Eds) are compiled in our official editorial document.
